The truth about free domains

No platform can register a real .com / .in domain for free, because registries charge fees. Even big hosting companies include a domain only with a paid plan. Here are your real options:

1. A free subdomain (right here)

yourname.{{ base }} is instantly free and comes with HTTPS. Sign up.

2. Your own domain, cheaply

New extensions (.xyz, .site, .online) often have a very cheap first year. Compare prices at a few registrars, check the renewal price, then connect it here.

3. Student and community offers

Some registrars give students or developers a free first-year domain (for example through the GitHub Student Developer Pack), and some community projects hand out free subdomains. Offers change, so check the current terms yourself.

4. Domain checker

Type any domain to see whether it is already registered (uses public RDAP data).

Testing without any domain

Locally, mysite.localhost works out of the box. On a server, BASE_DOMAIN=1-2-3-4.sslip.io (your IP with dashes) gives you free wildcard DNS for demos.
